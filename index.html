Getting there from Austria Center Vienna

  1. Exit the Austria Center Vienna and head to the Kaisermühlen–VIC U1 station, right next to the venue.
  2. Take the U1 in the direction of Oberlaa. Ride about 7 minutes (6 stops) to Stephansplatz.
  3. Exit toward Kärntner Straße and walk south for about 5–7 minutes. Johannesgasse 12 is just off Kärntner Straße, next to the Bettelstudent pub.
